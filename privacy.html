Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 15, 2026

PicFrame Master: Add Watermark (the "App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your data.

1. No Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, device identifiers, or usage data. Our App is designed to be fully functional without any internet connection.

2. Local Image Processing

The App requires access to your photo library to select images for editing and to save the processed results. All image processing is performed locally on your device. Your photos are never uploaded to any remote servers, and we have no access to them.

3. Permissions Use

  • Photo Library Access: Used exclusively to let you choose photos for adding frames/watermarks and to save the final output to your gallery.

4. No Third-Party Sharing

Since the App does not collect or transmit data, no information is shared with third-party services or advertisers.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via the developer contact information provided on Google Play.
